# Consent-first shopkeeper interview and task guide

**Status: not conducted.** No participants, percentages or saved-money claims exist.

Ask permission to discuss shop processes and record notes; no names, customer debts or audio should enter the public demo. Explain simulation and synthetic data before the trial. Participation is voluntary and can stop anytime.

Questions: How often is restocking cash constrained? How is the reserve chosen? Does supplier credit, minimum order, fee or pack size change the decision? How do you select urgent goods? What does partial delivery look like? Are lead times reliable? Would speaking customer debts aloud be acceptable? Which units/languages and noisy conditions are common? What current tool or notebook is used?

Uncoached tasks on synthetic data: setup one product/customer; mixed sale/part payment; correct a quantity; record repayment; ask stock/balance; plan with explicit budget/reserve/fee; exclude an item and revise budget; save unsent draft; receive only part at a changed price; recover a refresh. Stop and ask before rescuing; record rescue turns.

Record denominator, completion, failed/ambiguous turns, time, confirmation objections, privacy objections and recommendation objections. Distinguish parser error, transcription error and owner input error. Ask whether the proposal was useful and what it missed. Compare with their usual method only with consent and without calling this a controlled impact study. Publish de-identified actual observations and resulting changes, never invented scores.
