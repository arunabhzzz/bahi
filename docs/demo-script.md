# Demo script (under 3 minutes)

Use the language-model mode if you have a key (closest to Alexa+), otherwise the built-in parser. Keep the "synthetic demo data" banner visible. Start with **Fresh demo**.

1. **Record a credit sale (0:00–0:30).** "Sold three rice and two cheeni to Ramesh, he paid fifty." Bahi reads it back with the total and credit; say "haan". Point at stock and Ramesh's balance changing in the side panel.
2. **Fix a mistake (0:30–0:55).** "No, that was one sugar." Bahi reads the correction and the credit change; "yes". Then "That was Meena, not Ramesh" → "yes". The original stays in the history.
3. **Payment (0:55–1:10).** "Meena paid two hundred" → "yes".
4. **The analyst (1:10–2:10).** "Why were sales down on Tuesday?" The chart shows the gap by item; the answer names Ramesh and the sugar stock-out. Then "Who's a credit risk?" (Farooq) and "What's running low?".
5. **Decide what to buy (2:10–2:40).** "Plan budget 2000 reserve 500", "leave out biscuits", "save it" → "yes". Nothing is sent to a supplier.
6. **Close (2:40–3:00).** The architecture slide: Alexa+ → MCP → append-only ledger; all maths in code; every change confirmed.

For a Hinglish moment: "Ramesh ko do kilo cheeni, sau rupaye diye" → Bahi says to give ₹4 change.
